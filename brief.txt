PROJECT OVERVIEW
A modern, high-performance website for Nexitura — a technology and creative services company specializing in digital solutions for businesses of all sizes. The site must project confidence, technical expertise, and forward momentum, reflecting the Nexitura brand identity.
Design Principle: Bold. Technical. Forward-Moving. Built to convert.

DESIGN GUIDELINES
Overall Feel
Precise and technical — sharp lines, intentional whitespace
Bold without being loud — confident, not aggressive
Modern and future-oriented — reflects cutting-edge services
Visual Elements
Geometric shapes and grid patterns as background accents
Cyan-to-navy gradients used sparingly on hero sections and CTAs
Glassmorphism cards on service and pricing sections
Subtle particle or grid animation on the homepage hero
Icons: Outline-style, consistent stroke weight (Lucide, Phosphor, or similar)
Typography
Headlines: Bold, tight tracking, clean sans-serif (Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans)
Body: Regular weight, comfortable line height (1.6–1.8)
Code/tech elements: Monospace font for any technical labels or stack references
Keep copy minimal — strong headlines, short supporting text
Color Usage
Context
Color
Primary backgrounds
White #FFFFFF, Light Grey #F4F6FB
Section accents / hero BG
Deep Navy #1B2A6B
CTAs / Highlights
Cyan #00C8E0
Body text
Dark Charcoal #1A1A2E or #333333
Borders / dividers
Light Grey #CCCCCC or Cyan at 20% opacity
Interactions
Smooth scroll throughout
Subtle card hover: lift + soft shadow + cyan border accent
Buttons: Cyan fill, navy text; invert on hover
Page transitions: Simple fade or slide-up reveal
Counters or animated stats on About page (optional)
Mobile Requirements
Fully responsive — mobile-first approach
Hamburger menu with slide-in navigation panel
Stack all grid cards vertically
Portfolio filter bar collapses to dropdown on mobile
WhatsApp button fixed/floating on mobile for quick access
What NOT To Do
No dark-only theme • No cluttered layouts • No stock photography • No slow animations • No feature creep
